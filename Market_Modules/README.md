# LATAM Power Markets

Latin America features some of the world's most hydro-dominant and interconnected electricity grids, shaped by distinct regional geographies, heavy reliance on renewable baseloads, and evolving wholesale spot markets (such as Colombia's SIN and regional SIEPAC integrations). 

The regional modules standardize heterogeneous regulatory frameworks, asset registries, and transmission topologies into modular, data-driven analytical pipelines.
